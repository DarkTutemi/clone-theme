What makes BRONOIR shoes different from other barefoot shoes?

BRONOIR combines barefoot ergonomics with grounding technology using real copper. This allows you to not only move naturally and improve posture but also reconnect with the Earth’s energy, helping reduce inflammation, stress, and fatigue. Plus, every pair is designed with premium materials for both comfort and style.

How does the grounding (Earthing) function work?

Each BRONOIR shoe features a copper plug built into the sole that maintains direct contact with the ground. Copper is a natural conductor, allowing electrons from the Earth to flow into the body. This helps neutralize free radicals and supports overall well-being, especially when walking on natural surfaces like grass, soil, or sand.

Are BRONOIR shoes suitable for everyday wear?

Absolutely! BRONOIR shoes are designed for daily use—whether you're walking, working, traveling, or simply relaxing. The minimalist sole offers a barefoot experience while providing just enough protection, and the stylish design means you can wear them almost anywhere.

How should I choose the right size?

We recommend selecting your regular size for a barefoot fit. If you're between sizes or prefer a slightly roomier feel, consider sizing up. Our sizing guide on the product page can help, and our customer service team is happy to advise based on your foot measurements.

Can I wear BRONOIR shoes in wet conditions?

While BRONOIR shoes are made with high-quality materials, we recommend avoiding heavy rain or very wet conditions to maintain the integrity of the grounding copper and the sole. A little moisture won’t hurt, but they’re best suited for dry, natural surfaces to get the full grounding benefit.
